🧭 Introduction

Missing values are often missing as sets of variables. This implies that there is a common cause of missingness that tends to affect several variables simultaneously. This suggests that seeking to understand such a causal explanation may resolve many missing values.

An UpSet plot is a data visualization method used to show overlapping relationships and intersections between multiple sets. Here the sets are observations and the members are the variables that are missing.
